\begin{ChineseAbstract}
建筑群能源管理需要综合考虑天气、用电负荷、分时电价、光伏发电与室内热舒适度。在控制实验中，数据集、算法配置、模型权重和评估结果往往分散保存，难以追溯不同算法的运行条件及结果来源。本研究基于CityLearn设计并实现了建筑群能源管理与仿真评估平台，将社区数据整理、控制任务执行与逐小时结果诊断连接为可追溯的实验流程。

（一）实现社区数据管理与仿真数据集发布。平台按社区管理共享天气、电价和日历数据，按建筑管理负荷与设备参数，支持历史文件导入及测量数据上报。发布时自动完成时间戳对齐、单位换算、设备参数检查和建筑热动力学模型训练，将选定建筑的共同时间窗口打包为带有版本编号、来源记录及文件校验值的数据集。24步以内的试运行用于检查环境能否启动；历史轨迹上的一步预测拟合与控制策略变化后的长期闭环验证分别报告。

（二）实现多控制器任务编排与模型管理。平台使用Vue、Spring Boot、Python和MySQL连接参数配置、异步执行、模型检查点保存与关联评估，接入无控制基准、CHESCA分层控制、按建筑部署的强化学习控制及演员-注意力-评论家多智能体深度强化学习适配方法。关系数据库保存任务与配置，任务目录保存模型权重、指标、日志和逐小时动作轨迹。适配入口记录模型与数据的对应关系，先在训练窗口中筛选每栋建筑均满足舒适阈值的候选，再按平均电费选取检查点；未达标候选保留为诊断记录。

（三）实现多维评估对比与逐小时决策诊断。平台结合建筑及社区指标、能源流向、动作曲线和无控制基准初步评估定位异常时段，并提供以独立无控制基准为参照的舒适度费用综合评分。系统测试覆盖社区评估、任务运行与数据来源核查。在固定分档控制规则下，修复生活热水储能状态更新错误后，本地数据集的社区峰值下降，电费与碳排放略升。强化学习对比和历史六种子配对结果重算用于检验控制表现及尖峰计价奖励；关键时刻的储能轨迹则用于分析所检查策略的峰前储能不足现象。上述结果分别反映环境修复、奖励配置和具体运行轨迹，避免将其混为单一算法增益。

平台支持建筑群控制实验的数据发布、任务调度、结果对比和过程追溯，为核查控制表现及其运行条件提供了统一入口。

\ChineseKeywords{机器学习；建筑群能源管理；仿真评估；多智能体强化学习；实验管理；可视化诊断}
\end{ChineseAbstract}

\begin{EnglishAbstract}
Managing energy across a group of buildings requires balancing weather, electrical load, time-of-use electricity prices, solar photovoltaics, and indoor thermal comfort. In practice, scattered datasets, configurations, model checkpoints, and evaluation outputs make multi-algorithm experiments difficult to reproduce and audit. Built on the CityLearn simulation environment, this thesis designs and implements a building-cluster energy management and simulation evaluation platform that connects community data preparation, control task management, and hour-by-hour diagnostic analysis.

First, community data management and dataset publishing link shared weather, pricing, and calendar records with building-level loads and equipment parameters through file imports and measurement submissions. During publishing, timestamp alignment, unit conversion, equipment checks, and building thermal-dynamics model training produce versioned simulation datasets with source provenance and file hashes. Short rollout checks verify runtime compatibility, whereas one-step historical temperature fit is explicitly distinguished from counterfactual closed-loop control validation.

Second, Vue, Spring Boot, Python, and MySQL coordinate task configuration, asynchronous process execution, checkpoint archiving, and linked evaluation across the no-control baseline (NoControl), the Community-based Hierarchical Energy Systems Coordination Algorithm (CHESCA), per-building reinforcement learning methods (the single-agent algorithm SAC together with the multi-agent algorithms IPPO, MAPPO, and District-MARL), and Actor-Attention-Critic Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning (AAC-MADRL). Relational tables store metadata while task directories preserve models, metrics, logs, and action trajectories. The AAC-MADRL adapter enforces building-level comfort thresholds before selecting checkpoints by average training-window bill, retaining unqualified runs as diagnostic records.

Third, building and community metrics, energy-flow diagrams, step-wise action traces, and no-control baseline assessments support visual diagnosis, complemented by an independent-reference comfort-and-cost score. System tests cover community assessment workflows, task lifecycles, and data provenance. Under fixed graded peak-response rules, repairing the domestic hot-water storage update bug lowers local-dataset peak demand while slightly increasing electricity bill and carbon emissions. Learning-method comparisons and a reanalysis of archived six-seed paired results examine control performance and peak-period battery pricing. Separate trajectory inspection identifies insufficient pre-peak storage in the examined policy. These findings distinguish environment repairs, reward settings, and individual operating traces rather than attributing all changes to one algorithmic improvement.

The platform provides practical engineering support for organizing, comparing, and auditing building-cluster control experiments.

\EnglishKeywords{machine learning; building-cluster energy management; simulation evaluation; multi-agent reinforcement learning; experiment management; visual diagnostics}
\end{EnglishAbstract}
