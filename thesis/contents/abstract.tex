\begin{ChineseAbstract}
建筑群能源管理需要在节省住户电费、压低社区用电高峰和保障室内热舒适之间取得平衡。开展控制实验时，数据与结果分散保存，容易混淆各次运行所用的参数和模型，也难以解释汇总指标变化的原因。本研究基于CityLearn设计并实现了建筑群能源管理与仿真评估平台，将社区数据准备、控制器后台运行与逐时段过程诊断连接为完整的实验流程。平台按社区保存天气与分时段电价，按建筑保存用电功率序列和设备参数，支持历史文件导入与测量数据上报。发布时完成时间对齐和单位换算，为各楼生成下一时间步室温预测模型，经短时间试运行后保存为带版本号的数据集。系统采用Vue、Spring Boot、Python和MySQL，统一管理无控制基准、分层规则控制与多智能体强化学习任务，分别保存运行配置、模型权重和评估结果。本研究沿用社区分层能源系统协调算法（CHESCA）的分层控制框架，实现分级柔性约束能源控制（TFEC），并根据社区刚需用电功率的突增程度连续调节制冷动作。在多智能体强化学习方面，本研究为区域协同多智能体强化学习（District-MARL）设计社区观测与削峰奖励，并将演员-注意力-评论家多智能体强化学习（AAC-MADRL）等算法集成到平台，作为对照控制器。模型比较页面展示逐楼电费与冷热不适比例，以独立无控制基准计算综合评分，并提供用电功率和设备动作曲线，帮助快速定位异常楼栋与时刻。在2023年本地数据集上，固定分级柔性约束能源控制的连续调节配置后修复生活热水储罐的状态更新错误，社区最高用电功率由$12.705\text{ kW}$降至$12.119\text{ kW}$（下降$4.61\%$），电费与碳排放略增。强化学习对比和6种子历史结果重算显示，加入尖峰计价后的跨种子平均峰值比由$0.898116$降至$0.883651$。关键时段的电池曲线显示，所测试的学习策略在高峰前保留的电量较少。在线任务核查还发现动作执行顺序和模拟室温偏差会影响不同控制器之间的对比结论。平台功能与接口核验表明，已核验任务的页面指标能够与保存文件对应，为控制方法比较和异常排查提供了可靠的实验记录。

\end{ChineseAbstract}

\begin{EnglishAbstract}
Building-cluster energy management must balance electricity bills, community peak demand, and indoor thermal comfort. When data and results are stored separately, matching runs to their parameters and models and explaining changes in aggregate metrics becomes difficult. This thesis develops a CityLearn-based simulation and evaluation platform that connects community data preparation, controller execution, and time-step result inspection. The platform stores weather and time-of-use electricity prices at the community level and power demand series and equipment parameters for individual buildings, supporting file imports and measurement uploads. During dataset publication, it aligns timestamps, converts units, trains building-specific models to predict indoor temperature at the next time step, and saves versioned datasets after a short simulation check. Implemented with Vue, Spring Boot, Python, and MySQL, the system manages no-control baselines, hierarchical rule-based controllers, and multi-agent reinforcement learning tasks while preserving their configurations, model weights, and evaluation results. Tiered Flexible-Constraint Energy Control (TFEC) builds on the Community-based Hierarchical Energy Systems Coordination Algorithm (CHESCA). It continuously adjusts cooling actions according to increases in community non-shiftable power demand. Community observations and peak-reduction rewards are designed for district-level multi-agent reinforcement learning (District-MARL). The platform also integrates comparison controllers, including Actor-Attention-Critic Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (AAC-MADRL). The comparison interface presents building-level electricity bills and thermal discomfort proportions, computes a composite score relative to an independent no-control baseline, and provides power and action curves to locate differences across buildings and time steps. On the 2023 local dataset, repairing the domestic hot-water storage state update under a fixed configuration of the proposed controller reduces community peak demand from 12.705 kW to 12.119 kW (4.61\%), while slightly increasing cost and carbon emissions. Reinforcement learning comparisons and recalculated six-seed historical results show that the cross-seed mean peak ratio decreases from 0.898116 to 0.883651 with peak-period battery pricing. Battery trajectories at key time steps show that the inspected learning policy retains less charge before peak demand. Online task checks also identify action execution order and simulated temperature deviations as factors affecting result interpretation. Functional and interface checks show that displayed metrics match saved files for the inspected tasks, providing a reliable experimental record for controller comparison and fault diagnosis.

\EnglishKeywords{machine learning; building-cluster energy management; simulation evaluation; multi-agent reinforcement learning; experiment management; visual analysis}
\end{EnglishAbstract}
