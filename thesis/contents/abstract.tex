\begin{ChineseAbstract}
建筑群能源系统受到天气、住户用电、分时电价、光伏发电及室内热舒适需求的共同影响，具有跨建筑、跨时段耦合和多目标冲突的特点。如何结合规则先验与学习修正，并建立实验配置、模型、指标和轨迹的对应关系，是建筑群协同控制与实验管理需要解决的问题。

本文以CityLearn为仿真环境，围绕多智能体协同控制方法与可视化管理平台两条主线开展研究，主要工作如下。

（一）面向具备控制先验的社区，实现CHESCA-ResMARL受限残差多智能体控制框架。复现CHESCA的负荷预测、单楼温控、社区储能规划与动作回写流程，修复仿真环境中生活热水储能更新的容量引用问题，并实现依据不可调负荷突增程度调节动作的连续分级软门控。在此基础上，利用动作通道掩码、修正幅度和状态门控约束学习策略的修正，区分基线动作、请求修正与最终执行动作。通过环境修复对照、幅度消融和逐种子比较，分析削峰、费用、碳排与舒适度之间的关系。实验中出现小幅残差同时改善多项指标的个例，同一幅度在不同种子下的收益仍不稳定。

（二）面向直接学习控制与实验分析，实现区域协同观测、奖励机制及全栈可视化管理平台。通过环境包装器附加社区负荷和电池相对状态特征，以区域奖励及配对实验分析峰前储能准备与最终峰值之间的关系，并建立包含需量费用与电池吞吐折旧的情景核算模型。平台采用Vue、Spring Boot、Python与MySQL协同架构，贯通实验配置、训练与评估编排、模型复用、结果对比和逐时决策回放。关系库存储任务与配置等元数据，任务目录保存模型、日志、指标和运行轨迹；界面通过能源流向、购售电和设备动作视图支持结果核查。

本文形成了从控制动作生成到结果可视化诊断的工作流程，通过强基线上的残差对照与储能轨迹分析，为优化学习目标和评价协议提供了依据。

\ChineseKeywords{建筑群节能；多智能体强化学习；残差控制；区域协同；实验管理；可视化诊断}
\end{ChineseAbstract}

\begin{EnglishAbstract}
Building-cluster energy systems are influenced by weather, occupant demand, time-of-use electricity prices, photovoltaic generation, and thermal comfort requirements. Their operation involves coupling across buildings and time, together with conflicting objectives. Combining rule-based priors with learned corrections, and linking configurations, models, metrics, and trajectories, are central issues in cooperative control and experiment management.

Using CityLearn, this thesis studies multi-agent cooperative control and a visualization platform through two main lines of work.

First, CHESCA-ResMARL is implemented as a constrained residual multi-agent control framework for communities with an existing control baseline. The CHESCA workflow is reproduced, including load forecasting, local temperature control, community storage planning, and action write-back. A capacity-reference problem in the simulator's domestic hot-water storage update is repaired, and a continuous graded gate responds to surges in non-shiftable load. Learned corrections are constrained by action-channel masks, amplitude limits, and state-dependent gates. Baseline actions, requested corrections, and executed actions are recorded separately. Repair comparisons, amplitude ablations, and per-seed evaluations examine the relationships among peak demand, cost, carbon emissions, and comfort. A small residual improves several metrics in an individual run, while gains at the same amplitude remain inconsistent across seeds.

Second, district observations, reward mechanisms, and a full-stack visualization platform are implemented for direct learning control and experimental analysis. An environment wrapper adds district load and relative battery-state features. District rewards and paired experiments support analysis of storage preparation before peaks and final peak demand. A scenario accounting model includes demand charges and battery-throughput depreciation. The platform combines Vue, Spring Boot, Python, and MySQL to support configuration, training and evaluation orchestration, model reuse, result comparison, and hourly decision replay. Relational tables hold task and configuration metadata, while task directories preserve models, logs, metrics, and trajectories. Energy-flow, grid-exchange, and equipment-action views support inspection of experimental results.

The thesis connects action generation with visual diagnosis. Residual comparisons against a strong baseline and analysis of storage trajectories inform further refinement of learning objectives and evaluation protocols.

\EnglishKeywords{building cluster energy saving; multi-agent reinforcement learning; residual control; district coordination; experiment management; visual diagnostics}
\end{EnglishAbstract}
